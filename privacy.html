Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Xenith Macros handles information when you visit the Xenith Macros website or interact with services currently provided by Xenith Macros.

Xenith Macros respects your privacy and does not sell your personal information.

2. Information Collected Through This Website

The Xenith Macros website currently functions primarily as an information and product page.

At the current stage of the website, Xenith Macros does not operate a user account system, Discord login system, or built-in payment system on the website.

The website does not intentionally request sensitive personal information through forms or account registration.

3. Information You Provide Directly

If you contact Xenith Macros through Discord for support or a purchase, information you voluntarily provide may be visible to Xenith Macros.

Depending on the interaction, this may include information such as your Discord username, Discord user ID, messages, purchase details, or information needed to provide technical support.

This information is used for purposes such as customer support, purchase verification, software delivery, license management, and resolving technical issues.

4. Payment Information

Xenith Macros does not currently collect or store customers' debit-card or credit-card details through the website.

If payment is processed through a third-party payment provider, payment information is handled by that provider according to its own privacy policy and terms.

Future payment system: Xenith Macros may introduce direct website payments through Paystack in the future. If this functionality is implemented, this Privacy Policy will be updated to accurately explain what information is collected and how it is used.

5. License Information

When a customer purchases software that uses a license key, Xenith Macros may retain information necessary to associate the license with the customer's purchase.

This may include a license key, purchase information, and relevant customer or Discord information.

This information is used to provide access to purchased software, manage licenses, prevent unauthorized sharing, and provide customer support.

6. Discord

Xenith Macros currently uses Discord for customer communication, support, and software delivery.

Discord is a third-party service. Information shared through Discord is also subject to Discord's own privacy policy and terms.

Xenith Macros does not control how Discord independently processes information on its platform.

7. Website Analytics and Tracking

Xenith Macros does not currently intentionally use advertising trackers or sell visitor information to third parties.

However, website hosting providers, browsers, networks, or other technical services may process limited technical information as necessary to deliver and secure the website.

8. How Information Is Used

Information provided to Xenith Macros may be used for purposes including:

  • Providing customer support.
  • Confirming purchases.
  • Delivering purchased software.
  • Managing software licenses and license keys.
  • Responding to customer questions.
  • Preventing unauthorized use or distribution of software.
  • Maintaining and improving Xenith Macros services.

9. Information Sharing

Xenith Macros does not sell or rent customer personal information.

Information may be shared with third-party service providers when necessary to provide a service requested by the customer, such as payment processing, hosting, communication, or software delivery.

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

10. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide customer support, maintain purchase and license records, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, or meet applicable legal obligations.

Information that is no longer reasonably necessary may be deleted when appropriate.

11. Your Privacy Requests

If you have questions about personal information associated with your interaction with Xenith Macros, you may contact Xenith Macros through the official Discord server.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable requirements, you may request information about the personal information associated with your interaction, or request that information be corrected or deleted where appropriate.

12. Children's Privacy

Xenith Macros does not intentionally request sensitive personal information from children.

Users should not provide unnecessary personal information when contacting Xenith Macros.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Xenith Macros introduces new features, services, payment systems, or data-processing practices.

When changes are made, the updated version will be posted on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed.

14. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Xenith Macros handles information, please contact Xenith Macros through the official Discord server linked on the website.
