How to buy

Purchases are handled by hand for now, so please be patient.

  1. Join the Discord server and read the rules.
  2. Open a ticket in the tickets channel and ping the developer, telling them which macro you want.
  3. Pay using the payment link given in your ticket. Payment is confirmed manually.
  4. Get access. You receive a role that unlocks the download channel.
  5. Keep your ticket open. Download and unzip the app, then ping in the ticket to get your unique license key.

Anime Mysterious is different: buy the Donator[AM] gamepass, then type /verify in #bot-commands and the role comes automatically.

Never post your key publicly or share it. Staff will only give it to you inside your ticket.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you buy.

How do I get support?

Open a ticket in the Discord server. Support, license keys and bug reports are all handled there.

How do I receive my product?

After payment is confirmed in your ticket, you get a role that unlocks the download channel, and your own unique license key is sent to you in the same ticket.

What payment methods are accepted?

Payments are made through a secure payment link provided inside your ticket.

Are payments secure?

Yes. You pay on the payment provider's own page, so your card details are never shared with or stored by me.

Is it safe to use?

Automation is generally tolerated, and many games don't mind macros, but every game has its own rules and some don't appreciate automation. It is always a good idea to check the game's rules or ask the game's team before you use any macro. You use the tools at your own risk and are responsible for your own account.

What is the refund policy?

Because these are digital products with a personal license key, sales are generally final. If something doesn't work, open a ticket and I will do my best to fix it or make it right.

How long does an order take?

Orders are handled by hand, so it can take a little while, depending on when I am online. Open your ticket and I will get to it as soon as I can.

Can I share or resell my key?

No. Keys are personal. Shared or resold keys are revoked without a refund.

What do I need to run the macros?

A Windows PC. The standalone .exe versions need no extra software to be installed.
